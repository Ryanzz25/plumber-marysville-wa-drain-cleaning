Different clogs need different tools. Work through this list before you call so the right equipment comes on the first visit.

Only one sink or lavatory is slow

A single slow fixture usually starts at its trap and the vent. That is a small job, not a main line.

The toilet gurgles when the washer drains

That is a main line problem, not a P-trap. Two fixtures failing together is the point where a camera makes sense; sewer line repair explains what it can show.

Kitchen grease in long runs

Grease builds up in long horizontal kitchen lines and around disposals, especially in busy kitchens along State Avenue.

Hair in stacked bathrooms

Townhome baths stacked over finished rooms clog from hair. Floors underneath should be protected while the line is cleared.

Silt and leaves outside

Snohomish County rain packs outdoor drains with leaves, and storms bring silt toward Granite Falls.

Tell the plumber what you already tried

Say which store-bought snake or chemical opener you used, so tools are not mixed with leftover opener.

Snake, camera, then jet

A cable restores flow. Hydro jetting scours grease and scale from the pipe wall, but a line should be assessed before it is jetted, especially older clay. On many Marysville houses the order is snake first, camera if the clog returns, then jetting on a planned visit.
